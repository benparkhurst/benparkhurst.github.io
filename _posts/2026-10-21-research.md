Broadly, my research interests are in phonetics, phonology, documentation, language contact, and speech technology.

Specifically, I'm interested in the phonetics-phonology interface; description of languages and language varieties (especially contact varieties and underdocumented languages and varieties); how technology can facilitate the documentary process; the alignment of acoustic and articulatory evidence; and sociolinguistics (primarily sociophonetics) in underdocumented languages. I have also investigated some (morpho)syntactic phenomena in situations of language contact. As for my specific interests in phonetics, I like obstruents, vowels, prosody, and coarticulatory effects.
